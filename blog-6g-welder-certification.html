The Critical Importance of 6G Welder & Rigger Certification in Boilers

✍️ Target Enterprises Welding Division ⏱️ 7 Min Read 📅 September 2026 📍 Mechanical Fabrication Cell

High-pressure boiler tubes, superheaters, and main steam lines operate at temperatures surpassing 540°C and pressures exceeding 180 kg/cm². Under these severe parameters, a single microscopic defect in a weld seam can result in boiler explosion, catastrophic rupture, and months of plant outage.

1. Why the 6G Position is the Ultimate Test

Unlike 1G (flat) or 2G (horizontal) welding where the pipe can be rotated, the 6G position locks the pipe at a fixed 45-degree inclination. The craftsman cannot rotate the work piece and must weld continuously across flat, vertical, horizontal, and overhead orientations using both hands.

The 100% RT Standard: Under the Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) and ASME Section IX, boiler pressure welds undergo non-destructive Radiographic Testing (RT) and Ultrasonic Testing (UT). Zero root undercut, zero lack of penetration (LOP), and zero slag inclusion are permitted.

2. Welder Qualification Card (WQC) Verification

Every 6G welder mobilised by Target Enterprises undergoes physical coupon test welding on carbon steel (SA106 Gr.B) or alloy steel (P11, P22, P91) pipes under strict radiographic evaluation before being dispatched to site.

✔
Argon TIG (GTAW) Root Run: Flawless root pass ensuring uniform inner penetration and full keyhole fusion without excessive root concavity.
✔
SMAW Low-Hydrogen Hot Pass: Multi-run filling with E7018 or alloy-matched low hydrogen electrodes baked to 350°C.
✔
Precision Pre-Heat & Interpass Control: Continuous temperature monitoring using thermal chalks/pyrometers to eliminate hydrogen-induced cracking.

3. Certified Crane Rigging: The Other Half of the Equation

Erecting heavy boiler drums, economizer headers, and pre-assembled tube banks requires certified Riggers (Level 1 to 3) capable of calculating hitch angles, center of gravity (COG), and Safe Working Loads (SWL). A flawless weld joint cannot be made if the pipe alignment is strained by improper rigging slings.
