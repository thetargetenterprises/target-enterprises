🛡️ Industrial Safety & EHS Division

Factories Act Sec 40B Zero-Incident SOP

Frontline environmental health and hazard prevention teams ensuring strict compliance with Permit-to-Work (PTW) protocols, HIRA assessments, and continuous gas monitoring in high-pressure thermal, chemical, and petrochemical plants.

Safety Officer (EHS Lead)

ADIS / NEBOSH
Qualification (योग्यता): ADIS / RLI / CLI / NEBOSH IGC or B.Sc in Fire & Safety.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): PTW issuance, daily site audits, hazard reporting & mock safety drills.
Statutory Reference: Mandatory safety management under Factories Act 1948.

Safety Steward / Attendant

6-Month HSE
Qualification (योग्यता): Intermediate (10+2) with accredited 6-month industrial safety certification.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Confined space entry watch, hot work fire sentry & PPE verification.
Statutory Reference: Continuous toxic gas monitoring & emergency siren activation.

Fire Safety Officer & Crew

B.Tech / NFSC
Qualification (योग्यता): BE / B.Tech / B.Sc in Fire & Safety Engineering or NFSC Nagpur Course.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Hydrant ring main testing, foam tender operation & emergency evacuation.
Statutory Reference: State Fire Services Act & TAC compliance standards.

⚡ 6G Pipe & Pressure Vessel Welding

ASME Sec IX 100% RT Cleared

Master fabrication trades specialized in high-pressure alloy pipelines, superheater boiler coils, and cross-country hydrocarbon transit lines fixed at 45-degree inclinations without pipe rotation.

6G TIG & ARC Welder

WPQ Card
Qualification (योग्यता): ITI Welder trade with current Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) card.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Argon TIG root pass followed by low-hydrogen E7018 multi-pass hot fill.
Quality Standard: Zero porosity, zero undercut under Radiographic (RT) & Ultrasonic (UT) testing.

Mechanical Pipe Fitter

ITI Fitter
Qualification (योग्यता): ITI Fitter (NCVT) with 3+ years experience in heavy industrial piping.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Reading isometric drawings (P&ID), pipe beveling, root gap fit-up & tacking.
Quality Standard: Strict flange perpendicularity & bolt-hole orientation tolerances.

Gas Cutter & Grinder

Skilled Craft
Qualification (योग्यता): Trade tested with 2+ years field experience in structural cutting & grinding.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Oxy-acetylene & plasma cutting with clean root beveling and root-pass cleaning.
Quality Standard: Zero slag inclusion, uniform V-groove preparation ready for welding.

🏗️ Heavy Crane Rigging & Lifting Operations

SWL Certified Zero Incident

Engineering rigging crews specialized in heavy turbine erection, precast beam lifting, boiler drum hoisting, and multi-crane tandem lifts adhering strictly to load-slinging geometry.

Certified Rigger (L1–L3)

Rigger License
Qualification (योग्यता): Govt. certified rigging license or ITI Rigging with 3+ years heavy plant experience.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Calculating Safe Working Loads (SWL), D-shackle inspection & tandem lifting plans.
Safety Protocol: Strict adherence to center of gravity (COG) calculation & sling angle factor.

Crane Signalman / Marshall

Signal Certified
Qualification (योग्यता): Trained signalman with radio protocol and visual hand signal competency certification.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Directing blind crane maneuvers, tail-rope tag management & drop zone clearance.
Safety Protocol: Dedicated two-way radio channels & universal whistle emergency stops.

Scaffolding Crew & Lead

Cuplock / Tubular
Qualification (योग्यता): Certified scaffolder trained in BS EN 12811 standards with height fitness clearance.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Heavy-duty staging erection, cantilever scaffolding & green/red tag auditing.
Safety Protocol: 100% tie-off dual lanyards with full body harness inspection.

⚙️ Turnkey Plant Staffing & Engineering Crew

HT / LT Wireman Turnkey Batches

Comprehensive project site workforce capable of handling heavy foundation casting, switchyard electrical commissioning, instrument calibration, and complete shift operations.

HT / LT Industrial Electrician

Supervisor License
Qualification (योग्यता): ITI Electrician with State Electrical Licensing Board wireman/supervisor license.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Transformer termination, HT cable jointing, motor megger tests & control panel wiring.
Safety Compliance: Strict Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) and arc-flash protective gear adherence.

Civil Site Engineer & Lead

B.Tech / Diploma
Qualification (योग्यता): B.Tech or Diploma in Civil Engineering with plant civil infrastructure experience.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Mass concrete pouring, pile cap erection, structural shuttering & bar-bending auditing.
Safety Compliance: Total station survey layout verification & concrete cube compressive tests.

Instrumentation Technician

DCS / PLC Certified
Qualification (योग्यता): Diploma / ITI in Instrumentation with process transmitter calibration skills.
Core Responsibilities (मुख्य कार्य): Loop checking, RTD / thermocouple calibration, control valve actuator tuning.
Safety Compliance: Intrinsically safe junction box termination under hazardous area regulations.
