Trades & Eligibility Standards

Detailed requirements, qualifications, and operational responsibilities across all 4 industrial divisions.

🔥 Fire & Rescue Division

• Fireman: 10th/12th + 6-Mo/1-Yr Firefighting Course + Physical Fitness.

• Fire Supervisor: Sub-Officer Fire Course / Safety Diploma + 2y exp.

• Fire Technician: ITI Fire & Safety / Mechanical + Pump room servicing.

• Fire Safety Officer: BE/B.Tech/B.Sc Fire & Safety or PG Diploma.

🛡️ Industrial Safety (EHS)

• Safety Officer: ADIS / RLI / CLI / NEBOSH IGC or B.Sc Safety.

• Safety Supervisor: Diploma in Industrial Safety / Graduate + 2y field exp.

• Safety Steward: Intermediate (10+2) + 6-month Basic HSE Training.

• Safety Attendant: High School + Confined Space Sentry Certificate.

⚙️ Engineering & Administration

• Civil Engineer / Superv.: B.Tech / Diploma in Civil + Plant layout exp.

• Electrical Engineer / Superv.: B.Tech / Diploma Electrical + Supervisor License.

• Operations & HR Manager: MBA / PGDM in HR or Graduate + 5y plant exp.

• Computer Operator (MIS): DCA / BCA / O-Level + 35+ WPM typing & Excel.

⚡ Skilled Trades & Workforce

• Rigger: Certified Rigger Level 1, 2, or 3 / Heavy lifting experience.

• Fitter (Pipe / Struct): ITI Fitter trade certificate + 3+ yrs fabrication exp.

• Welder (TIG / MIG / 6G): ITI Welder + 6G / 3G Welder Qualification Card.

• Gas Cutter & Grinder: 2+ yrs industrial steel cutting & grinding exp.

• Industrial Helper: Physically fit, age 18-45, basic plant safety awareness.
