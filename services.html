🛡️ Industrial Safety & EHS Division

Factories Act Sec 40B Zero-Incident SOP DGMS / DIS Standards

Frontline environmental health and hazard prevention teams ensuring strict compliance with Permit-to-Work (PTW) protocols, HIRA assessments, and continuous toxic/flammable gas monitoring in high-pressure thermal, chemical, and petrochemical plants.

Safety Officer (EHS Lead)

ADIS / NEBOSH
Mandatory Qualification: ADIS (State Technical Board) / RLI / CLI / NEBOSH IGC or B.Sc in Fire & Safety.
Technical Scope: HIRA, daily JSA preparation, plant hazard auditing, mock emergency evacuation drills & TBT.
Statutory Compliance: Factories Act 1948 (Section 40B) statutory plant safety management.
Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours (Verified background & DIS certified).

Safety Supervisor & Auditor

SAFETY-SUP
Mandatory Qualification: Diploma in Industrial Safety / Graduate with 2+ years field inspection experience.
Technical Scope: Height work staging audits, excavation barricading, PPE enforcement & unsafe act reporting.
Statutory Compliance: Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) energy isolation & hot work safety clearance.
Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours with complete police clearance.

Confined Space Safety Sentry

HSE-SENTRY
Mandatory Qualification: 10+2 Intermediate with accredited 6-month industrial safety and first-aid certification.
Technical Scope: Vessel manhole standby watch, entrant logging, forced ventilation monitoring & siren alert.
Statutory Compliance: Calibrated 4-gas detector continuous atmospheric checking (O2, LEL, CO, H2S).
Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Transit (Ready Gangs).

Fire Safety Officer & Crew

FIRE-RESCUE
Mandatory Qualification: BE / B.Tech Fire & Safety or Sub-Officer Course from NFSC Nagpur / State Fire Academy.
Technical Scope: Hydrant ring main pressure tests, foam bowser operations, breathing apparatus (SCBA) drills.
Statutory Compliance: State Fire Prevention and Life Safety Measures Act & TAC regulations.
Deployment SLA: 48 Hours (Turnkey emergency ready squads).

⚡ 6G Pipe & Pressure Vessel Welding

ASME Sec IX IBR Certified 100% RT Pass Rate

Master pressure pipe welders specialized in high-pressure alloy pipelines, superheater boiler coils, and cross-country hydrocarbon transit lines fixed at 45-degree inclinations without rotation.

6G TIG & ARC Welder

ASME-IX / IBR
Mandatory Qualification: ITI Welder with current Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) card for 6G position.
Technical Scope: Argon TIG (GTAW) root pass followed by low-hydrogen E7018 multi-pass hot fill and capping.
Quality Standard: Zero porosity, zero undercut under 100% Radiographic (RT) and Ultrasonic (UT) testing.
Deployment SLA: Immediate (Pre-tested coupon passed welders).

Mechanical Pipe Fitter

P&ID-FITTER
Mandatory Qualification: ITI Fitter (NCVT) with 3+ years experience in heavy industrial process piping.
Technical Scope: Reading piping isometrics (P&ID), pipe beveling, root gap fit-up, tacking & flange alignment.
Quality Standard: Flange perpendicularity, center-line accuracy & zero weld gap distortion.
Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours.

Structural Steel Fabricator

STRUCT-FAB
Mandatory Qualification: Trade tested fabricator with structural drafting knowledge and 3+ years site experience.
Technical Scope: Heavy column erection, girder fit-ups, box section fabrication & base plate layout setting.
Quality Standard: Strict diagonal tolerance & plumb alignment as per IS 800 standards.
Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

Gas Cutter & Bevel Grinder

CUT-GRIND
Mandatory Qualification: Trade tested specialist in oxy-acetylene/plasma cutting with 2+ years field experience.
Technical Scope: Straight, circular, and bevel edge cutting with zero burrs, slag cleaning & root pass grinding.
Quality Standard: Uniform 37.5-degree bevel angle preparation ready for immediate 6G root welding.
Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Availability.

🏗️ Heavy Crane Rigging & Lifting Operations

SWL Certified Tandem Lift Specialists Zero Incident History

Engineering rigging crews specialized in heavy turbine erection, precast beam hoisting, boiler drum lifting, and multi-crane tandem maneuvers adhering strictly to load-slinging geometry and safety limits.

Certified Rigger (L1, L2, L3)

SWL-RIGGER
Mandatory Qualification: Govt. certified rigging license or ITI Rigging with 3+ years heavy plant lift experience.
Technical Scope: Calculating Safe Working Loads (SWL), D-shackle inspection, spreader beams & sling hitching.
Safety Protocol: Center of gravity (COG) calculation and load-angle stress factor compliance.
Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

Crane Signalman & Marshall

SIGNAL-MARSHALL
Mandatory Qualification: Certified signalman with radio protocol and visual hand signal competency certification.
Technical Scope: Directing blind crane maneuvers, tail-rope tag management & drop-zone clearance.
Safety Protocol: Dedicated two-way radio channels & universal whistle emergency stops.
Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours.

Certified Scaffolder & Lead

CUPLOCK-BS12811
Mandatory Qualification: Certified scaffolder trained in BS EN 12811 / IS 2750 with height medical clearance.
Technical Scope: Heavy-duty staging erection, cantilever scaffolding, green-tag audit & sole plate leveling.
Safety Protocol: 100% tie-off dual shock-absorbing lanyards and full-body safety harnesses.
Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Transit.

Heavy Equipment Operator

HYDRA-CRANE
Mandatory Qualification: Commercial heavy motor vehicle (HMV) driving license with crane operator certification.
Technical Scope: Operation of Hydra (14T-25T), Crawler Crane, JCB & Excavator in congested plant layouts.
Safety Protocol: Outrigger stability checks, safe load indicator (SLI) monitoring & daily boom inspection.
Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

⚙️ Turnkey Plant Staffing & Engineering Crew

CEA Licensed Turnkey Batches Substation Commissioning

Comprehensive project site workforce capable of handling heavy civil foundations, switchyard electrical commissioning, instrument loop testing, and complete plant shift maintenance.

HT / LT Industrial Electrician

HT-LT-WIREMAN
Mandatory Qualification: ITI Electrician with State Electrical Licensing Board HT/LT wireman or supervisor license.
Technical Scope: Transformer termination, HT cable jointing, motor megger testing, switchgear & PLC panels.
Safety Compliance: Lockout-Tagout (LOTO), arc-flash protective gear & earthing resistance testing.
Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours.

Civil Site Engineer & Lead

CIVIL-ENG
Mandatory Qualification: B.Tech or Diploma in Civil Engineering with heavy industrial infrastructure background.
Technical Scope: Mass foundation casting, pile cap erection, shuttering audits & bar-bending schedule (BBS).
Safety Compliance: Total station survey layout verification & concrete cube compressive test records.
Deployment SLA: 72 Hours.

Instrumentation Technician

DCS-CALIBRATE
Mandatory Qualification: Diploma / ITI in Instrumentation with process sensor transmitter calibration skills.
Technical Scope: Loop checking, RTD / thermocouple calibration, control valve actuator tuning & tubing.
Safety Compliance: Intrinsically safe junction box termination under hazardous area classification.
Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours.

Millwright Fitter & Mechanic

TURBINE-FITTER
Mandatory Qualification: ITI Millwright / Mechanical Fitter with 4+ years turbine and pump overhaul experience.
Technical Scope: Dial gauge laser alignment, gearbox bearing clearance, dynamic balancing & pump gland packing.
Safety Compliance: Vibration analysis limits & shaft run-out tolerances under ISO 10816.
Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.
