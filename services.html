🛡️ Industrial Safety & EHS Division
Factories Act Sec 40B Zero-Incident Protocol

Frontline environmental health and hazard prevention teams ensuring 100% compliance with Permit-to-Work (PTW) protocols, quantitative HIRA risk assessments, continuous toxic/flammable gas monitoring, and emergency rescue readiness in high-pressure thermal, chemical, and petrochemical complexes.

Safety Officer (EHS Lead)

ADIS / NEBOSH
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: ADIS (State Technical Board) / RLI / CLI / NEBOSH IGC or B.Sc in Fire & Safety.
🛠️ Technical Scope: PTW issuance, daily JSA preparation, plant hazard audits, mock emergency drills & TBT.
📜 Statutory Compliance: Factories Act 1948 (Section 40B) plant safety officer management code.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours (Verified background & DIS certified).

Safety Supervisor & Auditor

SAFETY-SUP
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Diploma in Industrial Safety / Graduate with 2+ years field inspection experience.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Height work staging audits, excavation barricading, PPE enforcement & unsafe act reporting.
📜 Statutory Compliance: Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) energy isolation & hot work safety clearances.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours with verified police KYC.

Confined Space Safety Sentry

HSE-SENTRY
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: 10+2 with accredited 6-month industrial safety and first-aid certification.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Vessel manhole standby watch, entrant logging, forced ventilation checking & siren alert.
📜 Statutory Compliance: Calibrated 4-gas detector continuous atmospheric sampling (O2, LEL, CO, H2S).
⏱️ Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Transit (Ready standby gangs).

Fire Safety Officer & Crew

FIRE-RESCUE
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: BE / B.Tech Fire & Safety or Sub-Officer Course from NFSC Nagpur / State Academy.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Hydrant ring main testing, foam bowser operations, breathing apparatus (SCBA) drills.
📜 Statutory Compliance: State Fire Prevention and Life Safety Act & TAC regulations.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 Hours (Turnkey emergency squads).

⚡ 6G Pipe & Pressure Vessel Welding
ASME Section IX 100% RT Cleared IBR Certified

Master pressure pipe welders and mechanical fitters specialized in superheater boiler tubes, high-pressure steam pipelines, and cross-country hydrocarbon transit lines fixed at 45-degree inclinations without rotation, requiring 100% radiographic quality joints.

6G TIG & ARC Welder

ASME-IX / IBR
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: ITI Welder with current Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) card for 6G.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Argon TIG (GTAW) root pass followed by low-hydrogen E7018 multi-pass hot fill and capping.
📜 Quality Standard: Zero porosity, zero undercut under 100% Radiographic (RT) and Ultrasonic (UT) testing.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: Immediate (Pre-tested coupon passed welders).

Mechanical Pipe Fitter

P&ID-FITTER
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: ITI Fitter (NCVT) with 3+ years experience in heavy industrial process piping.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Reading isometrics (P&ID), pipe beveling, root gap fit-up, tacking & flange alignment.
📜 Quality Standard: Strict flange perpendicularity, center-line accuracy & zero distortion.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours.

Structural Steel Fabricator

STRUCT-FAB
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Trade tested fabricator with structural drafting knowledge and 3+ years experience.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Heavy column erection, girder fit-ups, box section fabrication & base plate layouts.
📜 Quality Standard: Strict diagonal tolerance & plumb alignment as per IS 800 standards.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

Gas Cutter & Bevel Grinder

CUT-GRIND
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Trade tested specialist in oxy-acetylene/plasma cutting with 2+ years field experience.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Straight, circular, and bevel edge cutting with zero burrs & root pass grinding.
📜 Quality Standard: Uniform 37.5-degree bevel angle preparation ready for immediate 6G root welding.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Availability.

🏗️ Heavy Crane Rigging & Lifting Operations
SWL Certified Tandem Lift Specialists

Precision engineering rigging crews specialized in heavy turbine generator erection, precast beam hoisting, boiler drum lifting, and multi-crane tandem maneuvers adhering strictly to load-slinging geometry and safe working load (SWL) limits.

Certified Rigger (L1, L2, L3)

SWL-RIGGER
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Govt. certified rigging license or ITI Rigging with 3+ years heavy plant lift experience.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Calculating Safe Working Loads (SWL), D-shackle inspection, spreader beams & sling hitching.
📜 Safety Protocol: Center of gravity (COG) calculation and load-angle stress factor compliance.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

Crane Signalman & Marshall

SIGNAL-MARSHALL
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Certified signalman with radio protocol and visual hand signal competency certification.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Directing blind crane maneuvers, tail-rope tag management & drop-zone clearance.
📜 Safety Protocol: Dedicated two-way radio channels & universal whistle emergency stops.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 24 to 48 Hours.

Certified Scaffolder & Lead

CUPLOCK-BS12811
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Certified scaffolder trained in BS EN 12811 / IS 2750 with height medical clearance.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Heavy-duty staging erection, cantilever scaffolding, green-tag audit & sole plate leveling.
📜 Safety Protocol: 100% tie-off dual shock-absorbing lanyards and full-body safety harnesses.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: Immediate Batch Transit.

Heavy Equipment Operator

HYDRA-CRANE
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Commercial heavy motor vehicle (HMV) driving license with crane operator certification.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Operation of Hydra (14T-25T), Crawler Crane, JCB & Excavator in plant layouts.
📜 Safety Protocol: Outrigger stability checks, safe load indicator (SLI) monitoring & boom inspections.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 Hours.

HT / LT Industrial Electrician

HT-LT-WIREMAN
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: ITI Electrician with State Electrical Licensing Board HT/LT wireman or supervisor license.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Transformer termination, HT cable jointing, motor megger testing, switchgear & PLC panels.
📜 Safety Compliance: Lockout-Tagout (LOTO), arc-flash protective gear & earthing resistance testing.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours.

Civil Site Engineer & Lead

CIVIL-ENG
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: B.Tech or Diploma in Civil Engineering with heavy industrial infrastructure background.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Mass foundation casting, pile cap erection, shuttering audits & bar-bending schedules (BBS).
📜 Safety Compliance: Total station survey layout verification & concrete cube compressive test records.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 72 Hours.

Instrumentation Technician

DCS-CALIBRATE
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: Diploma / ITI in Instrumentation with process sensor transmitter calibration skills.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Loop checking, RTD / thermocouple calibration, control valve actuator tuning & tubing.
📜 Safety Compliance: Intrinsically safe junction box termination under hazardous area classification.
⏱️ Deployment SLA: 48 to 72 Hours.

Millwright Fitter & Mechanic

TURBINE-FITTER
🎓 Mandatory Qualification: ITI Millwright / Mechanical Fitter with 4+ years turbine and pump overhaul experience.
🛠️ Technical Scope: Dial gauge laser alignment, gearbox bearing clearance, dynamic balancing & pump gland packing.
📜 Safety Compliance: Vibration analysis limits & shaft run-out tolerances under ISO 10816.
