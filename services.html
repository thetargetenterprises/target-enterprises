🛡️ Industrial Safety & EHS Division

Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) officers form the core frontline defense in thermal power, petrochemical, and refining plants. Every operative deployed through Target Enterprises undergoes verification of Permit-to-Work (PTW) protocols, HIRA, and statutory gas-monitoring procedures.

  • • Safety Officer: Mandatory ADIS, RLI/CLI, or NEBOSH IGC qualification. Manages plant hazard inspections, daily safety drills, and incident prevention.
  • • Safety Steward / Attendant: Accredited safety training. Responsible for confined space entry watch, hot work sentry, and gas detector calibrations.
  • • Fire Safety Officer: Degree/Diploma in Fire & Safety Engineering. Leads emergency response and hydrant pump room maintenance.

⚡ 6G Pipe & Pressure Vessel Welding

6G position pipe welding demands full joint penetration with the pipe fixed at an inclined 45-degree angle without rotation. Deployed across boiler tubes, high-pressure steam lines, and hydrocarbon pipelines requiring 100% radiographic test (RT) clearance.

  • • 6G TIG & ARC Welder: Valid WPQ card certified under ASME Section IX or IBR (Indian Boiler Regulations).
  • • Mechanical Pipe Fitter: ITI Fitter certificate with 3+ years experience reading piping isometrics (P&ID), beveling, and flange fit-ups.
  • • Gas Cutter & Grinder: Skilled in oxy-acetylene/plasma cutting with zero edge burrs and root-pass bevel grinding.

🏗️ Heavy Crane Rigging & Lifting Operations

Precision rigging is critical for the zero-incident lifting of heavy turbines, precast girders, boiler drums, and structural steel assemblies. Riggers certified by Target Enterprises strictly adhere to load-slinging geometry and Safe Working Load (SWL) limits.

  • • Certified Rigger (Level 1, 2, 3): Trained in calculating hitch tensions, inspection of wire rope slings, D-shackles, and spreader bars.
  • • Crane Signalman / Marshall: Dedicated communication specialist directing mobile, crawler, and tower crane operators.
  • • Rigging Helpers: Physically fit technicians for chain block hoisting, ground leveling, and tag-line handling during heavy lifts.

⚙️ Turnkey Plant Staffing & Engineering Crew

Power plant erection, continuous process plant turnarounds, and EPC infrastructure require rapid multidisciplinary mobilization. We provide end-to-end personnel pools capable of handling civil foundations, electrical switchyards, and turbine alignments.

  • • Civil Site Engineers & Supervisors: Experience in heavy foundation casting, shuttering, and structural steel erection.
  • • Electrical Engineers & Technicians: Certified electricians with HT/LT supervisory wireman licenses for switchgear testing and cable termination.
  • • Turnkey Mixed Deployment: Integrated crews of 20 to 300+ personnel mobilizing complete with toolboxes and safety supervisors.
