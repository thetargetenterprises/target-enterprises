⚡ 6-Step Rapid Plant Mobilization Framework

Standard operating procedure designed to eliminate client downtime during turnkey shutdowns and major project commissioning.

STAGE 01

Requisition Audit & Scope

Detailed assessment of technical trade mix, duration, shift patterns, and site-specific safety gear standards.

STAGE 02

Trade Testing & Verification

Rigorous physical testing of 6G weld joints (RT), electrical wiring competency, and ADIS qualification audits.

STAGE 03

KYC & Police Gate Pass

Aadhaar identity verification, background police clearance checks, and plant entry biometric documentation.

STAGE 04

Medical & Fitness Screening

Comprehensive medical checks ensuring 100% fitness for working at height, confined space entry, and vision standards.

STAGE 05

Transit & Camp Deployment

Interstate batch transit, on-site camp settlement, safety PPE kit issuance, and local labor department intimation.

STAGE 06

24x7 EHS Site Supervision

Continuous attendance monitoring, shift roll-calls, daily toolbox talks, and full statutory PF/ESIC compliances.
